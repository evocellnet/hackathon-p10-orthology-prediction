# Review R1: prior work and domain (orthology methods)
Reviewer: independent prior-work/domain agent. Reviewed DESIGN.md draft v0 at T+0h26m by the wall clock (15:06 CET; START 14:40). Inputs: PROMPT.md, DESIGN.md, LITERATURE.md §1–3, 5, 7.4, 10, and ENVIRONMENT.md.

## Verdict
The progressive ancestral-merge paradigm is a defensible choice. It is linear in the number of merges, hierarchical by construction and free of any reference database, and the brief allows it. As written, though, the design re-states Hieranoid 2 and leaves out the fixes the field has already found for Hieranoid's known failures. Two of the four novelty claims are not new. The "gene order as a first-class signal" claim has direct precedents that are not cited.

## 1. Novelty claims: corrections needed
- **SYNERGY** (Wapinski, Pfeffer, Friedman & Regev 2007, *Bioinformatics* ISMB;) already walks the species tree bottom-up, merges orthogroups at each ancestral node, and uses **sequence similarity and synteny jointly**. This is the closest precedent for §2.1 and is missing from LITERATURE.md.
- **edgeHOG** (Bernard, Nevers, Dessimoz et al. 2025) propagates HOG-anchored adjacencies along the species tree at OMA scale (2,845 genomes). **AGORA** and **DeCo/DeCoSTAR** reconstruct ancestral adjacencies from gene trees. The "ancestral adjacencies carried up the tree" mechanism is therefore established. What is new is only that it feeds back into the merge decision, at QfO scale, with QfO/OrthoBench evaluation.
- **OrthoFFGC**, a family-free method that uses gene-order context (verify the exact mechanism), is in the QfO 2022 table (LITERATURE §7.4). It has high EC/GO precision and the **lowest recall** (SwissTree TPR 0.503). LITERATURE.md says "no method uses synteny with QfO results". That is not quite right, and this row is a warning that synteny-driven inference can cost recall.
- §2.2, "multiple diverse representatives (k-medoids)", is the OF3/SHOOT approach (k-means representatives on the MSA embedding) and is close to OMAmer's per-HOG k-mer tables. It is not a difference from prior work.
- §2.3, "in-paralog co-orthology plus unmatched passes up", is exactly what InParanoid/Hieranoid already do. Singletons are carried upward in Hieranoid too.
- §2.4: species-tree inference from sketches or ASTRAL-Pro is standard (OF STAG, OF3 ASTRAL-Pro).
- **Suggested honest framing:** "A Hieranoid/SYNERGY-type progressive merge, modernised with (a) a species-overlap repair of fissions, (b) multi-evidence merge scoring (sequence + ancestral adjacency + 3Di) and (c) explicit polytomy/HGT handling, evaluated against Hieranoid 2 as the direct ancestor."

## 2. Known failure modes the design re-invents, and solutions to borrow
1. **Fissions that propagate (Hieranoid's main recall loss; OF3 shows Hieranoid behind OF3 on OrthoBench).** Once two child-level HOGs are separate, nothing re-joins them. *Borrow:* a species-overlap test at every merge. When two child-HOGs from the same child both match a partner and their species sets barely overlap (SO below a threshold, cf. pbDOOD SO ≥ 0.1 and outlier removal; FastOMA's merging of complementary HOGs; OMA's split-gene handling), treat them as a **fission/fragment and repair** instead of calling a duplication. Also add a final **root-level rescue**: run a sensitive profile/3Di search among root HOGs and singletons, and merge complementary, homologous ones.
2. **Consensus drift.** Hieranoid's consensus sequences become chimeric or low-complexity at deep nodes. Use real member sequences only, with a capped and species-diverse sample per ancestral gene (FastOMA subsamples about 20). Keep "consensus" in S4 only as a negative control.
3. **Family delineation is entangled with orthology.** Every competitive scalable method separates the two: FastOMA (OMAmer/Linclust root-HOGs), OF3 (core OGs), pbDOOD (Pfam plus MMseqs2). The brief itself names a "protein family placement" sub-task, and RESEARCH_TREE item 3 lists it, but DESIGN has no S0. Add **S0: de novo family clustering** with DIAMOND DeepClust or a Linclust cascade. Benchmark it on OrthoBench RefOG fusion/fission. Use it as a **soft** constraint and candidate generator for the merges. Without S0, every deep merge has to find all homologs on its own, and that is where Hieranoid loses recall.
4. **Fusion and multi-domain proteins.** One fused gene matching two ancestral genes will either chain them together (a fusion, which is FastOMA's 3.5× excess) or force one choice. Require bidirectional coverage (OMA-style, about 60% of the shorter sequence). Flag a representative whose hits to two targets are **non-overlapping** as a fusion candidate, and never merge transitively through it (Broccoli's chimera rule).
5. **Pseudo-orthology after differential loss** (teleost 3R, yeast WGD). Sequence cannot fix this. Synteny can (YGOB/Scannell et al. 2006 logic). This is the use case where the synteny layer should be **pre-registered to win**, tested on zebrafish/Tetraodon in OrthoBench and on Saccharomycetaceae in QfO.
6. **Positional vs phylogenetic orthology** (Dewey 2011). QfO SwissTree/TreeFam-A count co-orthologs as true. Synteny used to prune many-to-many down to "the" positional ortholog will **cost recall**. Restrict synteny to (i) choosing between competing HOG assignments and (ii) fission repair. Do not use it to drop in-paralogs. Tandem duplicates share their neighbourhood, so synteny carries no information for them and must abstain.
7. **Polytomies.** The NCBI taxonomy for QfO has multifurcations, and Hieranoid needs a binary tree. Arbitrary binarisation creates spurious HOG levels. Do a **k-way merge at polytomies**, or binarise internally while emitting OrthoXML levels only for real taxonomy clades.
8. **HGT and guide-tree discordance.** 30 of the 78 QfO species are prokaryotes. OrthoBench, the main dev set, has no prokaryotes and no HGT. The development subset of QfO must include a bacterial clade. Report STD-Bacteria and G_STD2_Luca separately so that HGT effects are visible and not averaged away.
9. **Rate heterogeneity** (worm, Ciona, fly). Raw bitscore best-match picks the wrong paralog. Borrow OF's length and distance normalisation of scores, or OMA's third-species witness rule: the sibling-of-parent ancestral proteome is a natural outgroup, as in InParanoid's outgroup option.

## 3. Benchmarks and evidence
- **The primary comparator must be Hieranoid 2** (public QfO row: SwissTree 0.726/0.950, STD-Euk RF 0.034), together with InParanoid5. Pre-register the claim "sequence-only Ancestra ≥ Hieranoid 2 on SwissTree/TreeFam-A F and OrthoBench F".
- **VGNC circularity.** VGNC/HGNC ortholog assignments lean heavily on Ensembl Compara plus synteny (HCOP), so a synteny-driven gain on VGNC is partly circular. S5 has to show its effect on SwissTree, TreeFam-A or OrthoBench as well, not only on VGNC.
- The **linear-scaling claim** must be stated as an empirical result with the measured ancestral-proteome size per node. For open prokaryotic pan-genomes, "unmatched passes up" makes the root proteome grow with S. Report total merge cost against S and the critical-path depth (ladderised taxonomy trees limit parallelism).
- **OrthoBench is development data.** Report it as such. Pairs exported from HOGs must use the standard speciation-LCA rule (verify with pyham on toy cases).

## 4. Verification
Use an established simulator instead of writing one: **Zombi** (Davín et al. 2020, *Bioinformatics*; Python; gene order, inversions, transfers, reconciled trees) or **ALF** (Dalquen et al. 2012; includes fusion/fission and LGT). A simulator written by the method's own team builds in the method's assumptions, so its "positive control" proves little.

## 5. Structure (3Di)
FoldTree's gain is concentrated in highly divergent families, and Fullmer et al. report a weak gain for short or α-helical proteins. Restrict 3Di to **deep-node unmatched or ambiguous representatives and to S0 rescue**, so the ProstT5 cost scales with the number of representatives and not with N. Pre-register a recall gain at deep levels (fewer OrthoBench fissions) as the hypothesis, not a gain in overall precision.
